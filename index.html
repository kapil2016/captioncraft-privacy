Privacy Policy

CaptionCraft is made by APPCRAFTSTUDIO. It writes captions for your videos and audio, and lets you edit and export videos. This page explains what leaves your phone when you use it, why, and for how long it is kept.

The short version

  • Your videos, photos and music stay on your phone. Editing and exporting happen on the device. Finished videos are saved to your gallery (Movies/CaptionCraft).
  • Only when you tap Auto captions is the sound of the part you chose sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI, which turns the speech into words. The picture is never sent.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your information for advertising.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, name or email address.

What leaves your phone, and why

Sound for captions When you ask for auto captions, the app cuts the audio of the chosen part into pieces of about four minutes and sends them over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server, which passes each piece to the Google Gemini API to transcribe. The piece is deleted from Gemini's file storage as soon as the words come back. Our server does not keep the sound.
The words that come back The transcribed words and their timings are kept on our server for 14 days, linked only to your app's random device token, so a retry after a lost connection, or captioning the same part again, is not charged twice. After 14 days they are deleted automatically.
Caption text for translation If you translate captions, the caption lines are sent to our server and to Gemini to be translated. We do not store the text; we only count how many translations each device makes per day, to apply a daily limit.
A random device token On first launch our server issues a random token that holds your caption minutes balance. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
A hashed phone identifier The app sends a one-way SHA-256 hash of Android's ANDROID_ID for this app. It is used only so the 5 free minutes are given once per phone, and so a reinstalled app gets its own balance back. The original ID cannot be recovered from the hash, and it is not used for advertising.
Purchase records When you buy caption minutes, Google Play gives the app a purchase token. Our server checks it with Google and keeps the order ID, the product bought, the purchase token and the date, so the minutes are credited once and can be restored.
Your IP address Seen by our server when the app connects, as with any internet service. When a new device registers, we keep only a keyed hash of the address and the day, to stop abuse of the free minutes; it is deleted the next day. We do not store IP addresses or use them for location.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts our server and its database (Cloudflare Workers and D1). See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini API turns speech into words and translates captions. Some requests are handled under Google's unpaid Gemini API service; for those, Google's terms allow it to use the content it receives to provide and improve its products, which can include review by trained people. Please do not caption recordings containing information you would not want processed this way. See the Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play handles purchases and gives us anonymous, aggregated statistics such as install counts and crash reports, under Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • The videos, audio and photos you pick. The app reads them through Android's own picker, only the files you choose, and never uploads them.
  • Your caption edits while you work, and the device token that holds your minutes, in the app's private storage.
  • The videos you export, saved in your gallery under Movies/CaptionCraft.

App data is not included in Android backups. Uninstalling the app removes everything in its private storage; exported videos stay in your gallery until you delete them.

Permissions

InternetTo make captions and translations, and to check purchases.
Google Play billingTo buy caption minutes.
Storage (Android 9 and older only)To save finished videos to your gallery. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.

CaptionCraft does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

How long we keep it

  • Transcribed words: 14 days.
  • Registration abuse checks: until the next day.
  • Device token, hashed phone identifier, minutes balance and purchase records: for as long as the balance may be used or restored, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can use every feature except auto captions and translation without anything leaving your phone. To have the data our server holds about your device deleted, email us at the address below. Because we do not know who you are, include the Google Play order ID of a purchase (from your Google Play receipt email) or tell us roughly when you installed the app, and we will delete the matching records within 30 days. Deleting a device's records also removes any unused caption minutes.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server, and between our server and Google, is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to the server and its database is restricted to the developer.

Children

CaptionCraft is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app's caption service, contact us and we will delete the related records.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
